Scoring key (1 = lowest, 5 = highest)

Criterion12345
Due date
entered as a date, converted to a score		No deadline, or 31+ days out15–30 days out8–14 days out4–7 days out3 days or less, or overdue
OpportunityOne-off or small request; no pipeline attachedSingle early-stage prospect; interest not yet qualifiedQualified prospect with beta or campaign potentialStrategic account, or reusable across several clients or verticalsHigh-value or live beta client; likely to close or expand
Impact
calculated		Adds points for: expected client outcome (exploratory 0, small test 1, planned campaign 2, large or live flight 3); likelihood to sign up for Latis (unlikely 0, possible 1, likely 2, committed 3); relationship (first meeting 0, 2–3 meetings +1, 4 or more +2); and previous pulls done (+1). Score = 1 + total ÷ 2, rounded down, max 5.
Effort
calculated, 5 = most effort		Starts at 1. +1 for 2–3 segments, +2 for 4 or more. +1 if an overlay is needed (Epsilon, Experian, etc.). +1 for a conquest rule (exclude the brand's site). +1 for a win-back rule (used to visit, then left). Capped at 5.

Value = average of due-date score, Opportunity and Impact (3 or higher counts as high). Priority = (due-date + Opportunity + Impact) ÷ Effort. Effort of 3 or lower counts as low. Ties go to the higher due-date score.
